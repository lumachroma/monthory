# Monthory Product Manifesto

## Identity

**Monthory**

**Your financial journal.**

The name combines **Month + Story**.

Every month tells a story. Monthory helps you remember it.

## Purpose

Monthory is a calm, private place to journal your financial life.

It is not an accounting system, budgeting coach, or conventional expense tracker.

It helps you:

- capture what happened
- reflect on where your money went
- understand patterns
- make deliberate adjustments
- build a sustainable habit of financial awareness

## Mission

> To make financial awareness a sustainable monthly habit.

## Vision

> To become the simplest and most personal financial journal—one that people genuinely enjoy returning to, month after month, year after year.

## Philosophy

> Observe first. Improve second.

Monthory does not tell users how to spend. It provides a clear, un-opinionated view of their financial life so they can decide what to do next.

## Product principles

### Journal First
Monthory is a journal before it is a finance app. Capture moments, not just transactions.

### Sustainable by Design
The best financial system is the one a person will still use years from now. Reduce friction and encourage consistency.

### Progress over Perfection
Life changes. Income changes. Spending changes. Monthory helps users understand the month they had, not judge it.

### Private by Default
Financial data belongs to the user. The initial product is offline-first and stores data locally.

### Intentionally Simple
Every feature must earn its place. Prefer clarity over feature volume.

### One Month at a Time
A month is a chapter. A year is a collection of chapters.

## Brand personality

Monthory is:

- calm
- honest
- personal
- private
- minimal
- thoughtful
- intentional

Monthory is never:

- loud
- judgemental
- pushy
- complicated
- anxiety-inducing
- needlessly gamified

## Design language

The experience should feel like opening a notebook rather than opening accounting software.

Prefer:

- comfortable spacing
- readable typography
- quiet visual hierarchy
- restrained colour
- meaningful summaries
- simple interactions

Avoid:

- guilt-oriented messaging
- aggressive alerts
- excessive badges
- noisy dashboards
- unnecessary animation
- feature-heavy workflows

## North Star

> Awareness over anxiety.  
> Sustainability over intensity.

## Product test

Every feature, screen, notification, calculation, and interaction should answer:

> Does this make financial journaling more sustainable?

If yes, consider it.

If no, leave it out.
